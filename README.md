# Touhou 18 ~ Unconnected Marketeers APWorld

This is an implementation of Touhou 18 for [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago)<br />

## What to Expect
This randomizer starts you off with only Reimu with only access to Stage 1 Lunatic with no resources. As you progress throughout the randomizer you will gain access to new stages, characters, and resources to make progression easier.

Cards are separated by purchasing and receiving. When you purchase a card in a shop, it is sent as a location but will not be active until received as an item in which case it can then be purchased or equipped as an initial card and act normally. 

Cards which are needed to be unlocked, being the protagonists' focus shot cards and Gluttonous Centipede, will send their locations upon reaching the conditions to unlock them in the game normally rather than being purchased in a shop.

Blank Card and Sky-Blue Magatama are acquired after receiving a certain number of cards decided via options. 
The Sky-Blue Magatama is required to access extra stage on top of the extra stage unlock.

## How To Setup Randomizer

**Remember to backup your scoreth18.dat located in AppData/Roaming/ShanghaiAlice in order to not lose your save data**

To play this implementation, you must be connected to an Archipelago multiworld room.

Connect to the IP of the room and put in your slot name that you used for your '.yaml' generation to connect to the server.

After, the client will say "Connecting to Touhou 18" as it searches for the game. The initial connection can only occur while you are in the main menu.

When you see "Beginning Main Loops" the client has successfully connected to the process and you can begin playing the randomizer.
